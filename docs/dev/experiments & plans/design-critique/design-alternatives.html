10 Colour doing shape's job

The accent marks selection, activity, importance, series-ness and the primary CTA — five jobs, one hue — while --color-danger marks both “delete forever” and “take out of this week”.

Current
Selected, active and “this is a series” all read identically; ✕ and Delete both read as danger.
A shape carries state
Recommended. A tick on selected chips, an underline on active sort, ✕ neutral until hover.

The current design in greyscale: selection state is nearly invisible, and ✕ stops being a warning.
